Hôtel Minuit

A hidden object and mystery game for iPhone and iPad. By Chouette Games.

1926, in a grand palace hotel. It is past midnight, and the Countess's pearl necklace has vanished from Suite 12. Search the hotel's rooms, find the hidden objects, gather the clues and name the culprit… then restore the palace to its former glory.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my game back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Your game is kept on your iCloud account. On the new device, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your investigation comes back on its own (it can take a minute). If the new device already has an investigation in progress, the game asks you which one to keep.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game completely, then open it again while connected to the Internet: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered when the game reopens. If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment details). For a refund, Apple decides: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have no candles left

Each search uses one candle, and a candle comes back every 6 minutes (up to 10). For the first two days, they are unlimited. You can also get 5 in the shop, with coins earned by playing. Nothing is lost: the investigation waits for you.

I can't find an object

Tap the lens: it shows where an object from the list is hiding. You start with 3, and the shop sells more for coins earned by playing.

How do I change the language, or turn off sound or reminders?

Tap the Settings button at the top of the lobby: language, sound, the reminder and statistics are all there.

How do I turn off game statistics?

Settings button in the lobby → "Statistics: off". See the privacy policy.

Ads

There are only ads if you choose them: for one candle right away when you have none left, or to double the coins from a search. Never a banner, never a forced ad.
