Night Train

A gentle solitaire for iPhone and iPad. By Chouette Games.

An old night train sets off from Paris to Istanbul. Win solitaire deals to travel from station to station, restore the carriages, get to know the passengers… and find out who booked compartment 7.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my game back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Your game is kept on your iCloud account. On the new device, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your journey comes back on its own (it can take a minute). If the new device already has a journey in progress, the game asks you which one to keep.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game completely, then reopen it while connected to the Internet: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered when the game reopens. If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment details). Refunds are decided by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have no tickets left

A ticket comes back every 30 minutes. You can also refill them in the shop, with coins earned by playing.

How do I change the language, or turn off sound or reminders?

Tap the Map tab at the bottom of the screen: the language, sound, reminders and statistics buttons are at the bottom of the panel.

How do I turn off game statistics?

Map tab → "Statistics: off". See the privacy policy.

Ads

There are only ads if you choose them: for 3 more cards when a deal is stuck, or to double the coins of a deal you won. Never banners, never forced ads.
