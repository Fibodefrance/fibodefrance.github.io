Petit Paris

A merge and cooking game for iPhone. By Chouette Games.

Lucien closed his bistro years ago. Help him reopen it: merge ingredients, cook for the regulars, choose the menu every day, and restore the counter, the terrace and the dining room.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my game back on a new iPhone?

Your game is kept on your iCloud account. On the new iPhone, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your game comes back on its own (it can take a minute). If the new iPhone already has a game in progress, the game asks you which one to keep.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game completely, then reopen it while connected to the Internet: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered when the game reopens. If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment details). Refunds are decided by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off music, sound effects or reminders?

Tap the gear at the top left of the game (it appears once the counter has been restored).

How do I turn off game statistics?

In the same gear menu: "Game statistics (no name, no account)". See the privacy policy.

Ads

There are only ads if you choose them, in the shop, for a little energy or to finish the cooking. Never banners, never forced ads.
