Tea Garden

Comfortable mahjong for iPhone and iPad. By Chouette Games.

Welcome to the Tea Garden. Madame Lin, the gardener, hands you a corner of garden to bring into bloom… one mahjong board at a time. Tap two matching free tiles: they fly away. Clear the board, and the garden grows.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my garden back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Your garden is kept on your iCloud account. On the new device, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your garden comes back by itself (it can take a minute). If the new device already has a garden started, the game asks you which one to keep.

The tiles are too small, or hard to tell apart

Tap Comfort on the home screen: three tile sizes, a design "with a number", high contrast, dimmed blocked tiles, and a left-handed layout.

I'm stuck

The shuffle is free when no pair is left, and the board can always still be cleared. A hint (10 coins) shows a pair, undo (5 coins) takes back the last move. Coins are earned by playing; when you are short, an optional short video gives a hint.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game completely, then open it again while connected to the Internet: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered on reopening. For "No ads" on a new device: the shop (the "+" next to the coins) → "Restore purchases". If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment details). For a refund, Apple decides: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

From the third day, a short ad may play between two boards: never during a board, at most one every three boards. The "No ads" purchase removes it for good. The other videos (double the coins, a hint) only play if you choose them. Never any banners.

How do I change the language, mute the sound or turn off statistics?

Tap Comfort on the home screen: language, soft sounds and statistics are at the bottom of the panel. See the privacy policy.
