Tea Garden

Privacy Policy

Applies to the game Tea Garden ("Tea Garden: Mahjong" on the App Store) on iPhone and iPad, all versions. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

In short

Tea Garden has no account and no sign-up. It sends its publisher game statistics with no name and no account, which you can turn off. Ads are provided by Google: from the third day of play, a short ad may play between two boards (never during a board, at most one every three boards), and the "No ads" purchase removes it for good; rewarded videos only open if you choose them. Your garden stays on your device, and a copy goes to your own iCloud account. Nothing is sold.

The game identifier does not identify a person or a device. It is a number drawn at random by the game the first time it opens. It does not come from any identifier of your device, your Apple account or advertising. Deleting the game deletes it.

What leaves the device

The iCloud backup

If your iPhone or iPad is signed in to iCloud, a copy of your garden is stored in iCloud, on your Apple account, so you can find it again on another device or after reinstalling. It is your space, not ours: we have no access to it. If two different gardens exist (two devices), the game asks you which one to keep, and the other one is set aside on the device: nothing is overwritten without asking you. To delete this copy: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Game statistics

To find out whether players come back, and where the game loses them, Tea Garden sends our server a few events, with no name and no account: the first launch, each day you open the game (once a day), steps of the game (each board won, the first daily challenge won, a trophy, the first item planted, a garden corner finished, the first time stuck, the first hint, the first undo, the first paid shuffle, the first time short of coins, a tile tapped several times in a row, a comfort setting changed, a language chosen, opening the shop, a purchase — without its amount —, an ad seen) and a few play times (2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 minutes). Each one carries the number of days since installation and its date, the platform, the game version and its language. When offline, they wait in your game and are sent later.

What links them: the randomly drawn game identifier (see above). Nothing else: no name, no contact details, no location, no device model, no IP address in our tables. We only read these statistics grouped together, by day of installation ("how many come back the next day?"), and cannot find yours. Because one identifier links your events together, they are not anonymous but pseudonymous.

Where and for how long: at Supabase (servers in Ireland, in the European Union), 13 months, after which the database deletes them automatically.

Why: to improve the game, on the basis of its publisher's legitimate interest in knowing whether people enjoy it. Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.

Turning them off: Comfort button on the home screen → "Statistics: off". Once off, nothing more is sent, and whatever was waiting in your game is deleted.

Server time

So that the daily challenge and the calendar do not depend on the device clock, the game asks our server for the time, at launch and each time you come back to the game. It sends nothing about you.

In-app purchases

They go through Apple, which collects the payment: we receive neither your name, nor your contact details, nor any payment data. The game keeps on the device the number of each delivered purchase, so it is never delivered twice.

Advertising

Tea Garden shows ads provided by Google AdMob, of two kinds:

  • A short ad between two boards, from the third day of play: never during a board, at most one every three boards. The "No ads" purchase (in the shop) removes it for good.
  • Optional rewarded videos: double the coins of a cleared board, or a hint when you are short of coins. They only open if you tap their button.

There are never any banners.

Your consent, where the law requires it (European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, some US states): Google's window asks you for it. At each launch, Google's kit checks this choice and, if it allows, prepares an ad in advance.

What Google collects to show an ad, measure it and fight fraud: the IP address (and so an approximate location), information about the device and the app, interactions with the ad, and diagnostic data. Ads are requested non-personalised, and the game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission: it has no access to the advertising identifier. Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Changing your choices: where the law provides for it, a "My ad choices (privacy)" line appears in the shop; it reopens Google's window.

Children

The game is not specifically aimed at children and asks them for no information. It contains in-app purchases: Screen Time on iPhone or iPad can block them.

Your rights

The European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over your personal data.

  • Game statistics carry no name, no contact details and no account, and we do not know which ones are yours: we therefore cannot hand them over or delete them. To stop any more being sent, turn them off in the game's settings; those already sent delete themselves after 13 months.
  • Your garden: deleting the game deletes it from the device; the iCloud copy is deleted in the iCloud settings.
  • Advertising data: change your choices in Google's window; your rights are exercised with Google, at myaccount.google.com.

You can also write to us at any time at legionduparadis@gmail.com; we reply within 5 working days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, or with the authority of your country.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date. A change in what the game sends would be announced in the game before it takes effect.
