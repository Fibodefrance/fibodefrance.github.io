Lighthouse Coast

Privacy Policy

Applies to the game Lighthouse Coast on iPhone and iPad, all versions. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

In short

Lighthouse Coast has no account and no sign-up. It sends its publisher game statistics with no name and no account, which you can turn off. Ads are optional and provided by Google: none is shown unless you choose to watch it. Your game stays on your device, and a copy goes to your own iCloud account. Nothing is sold.

The game identifier does not identify a person or a device. It is a number drawn at random by the game the first time it opens. It does not come from any identifier of your device, your Apple account or advertising. Deleting the game deletes it.

What leaves the device

The iCloud save

If your iPhone or iPad is signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game is stored in iCloud, on your Apple account, so you can find it on another device or after reinstalling. It is your space, not ours: we have no access to it. If two different games exist (two devices), the game asks you which one to keep, and the other is set aside on the device: nothing is overwritten without asking you. To delete this copy: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Game statistics

To know whether players come back, and where the game loses them, Lighthouse Coast sends our server a few events, with no name and no account: the first launch, each day you open the game (once a day), each return to the game during the same day (numbered), the steps of the game (each lighthouse lit, each new coast, each keeper's arrival, the first storm, the first evening fires, the first harvest, the first letter read, opening the shop, a purchase — without its amount —, an ad watched, reminders turned on, a game restored from iCloud) and a few play times (2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 minutes). Each carries the number of days since installation and the installation date, the platform, the game version and its language. Offline, they wait in your game and are sent later.

What links them: the randomly drawn game identifier (see above). Nothing else: no name, no contact, no location, no device model, no IP address in our tables. We only read these statistics grouped by installation day ("how many come back the next day?"), and cannot find yours. Because one identifier links your events together, they are not anonymous but pseudonymous.

Where and for how long: at Supabase (servers in Ireland, in the European Union), for 13 months, after which the database deletes them on its own.

Why: to improve the game, on the basis of its publisher's legitimate interest in knowing whether players enjoy it. Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.

Turning them off: tap the keeper's portrait at the top of the screen (the keepers window) → "Statistics: off". Once off, nothing more is sent, and whatever was waiting in your game is deleted.

Server time

So that the time counted while you are away, the evening fires and the storms do not depend on the device's clock, the game asks our server for the time, at launch and each time you come back to the game. It sends nothing about you.

In-app purchases

They go through Apple, which collects the payment: we receive neither your name, nor your contact details, nor any payment data. The game keeps on the device the number of each purchase delivered, so it is never delivered twice.

Reminders

The two reminders (your harvest waiting at the harbour, the evening fires at 6 pm) are off at first: you turn them on if you want to, and iOS then asks for your permission. They are notifications scheduled on the device itself: nothing goes through a server.

Advertising

Lighthouse Coast offers optional rewarded ads (doubling the harvest from your time away, or receiving the cargo from the open sea in the shop; at most three of each per day), provided by Google AdMob. None opens on its own: you choose them by tapping their button. There are no banners and no ads between screens. The no-ads pass gives the same gifts without any ad.

Your consent, where the law requires it (European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, some US states): Google's window asks for it the first time you tap an ad. At each launch, Google's kit checks this choice and, if it allows, prepares an ad in advance.

What Google collects to show an ad, measure it and fight fraud: the IP address (hence an approximate location), information about the device and the app, interactions with the ad, and diagnostic data. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and the game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission: it has no access to the advertising identifier. Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Changing your choices: where the law provides for it, a "My ad choices" button appears in the shop; it reopens Google's window.

Children

The game is not specifically aimed at children and asks them for no information. It contains in-app purchases: Screen Time on the iPhone or iPad can block them.

Your rights

The European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your personal data.

  • Game statistics carry no name, no contact and no account, and we do not know which ones are yours: so we can neither hand them over nor delete them. To stop sending them, turn them off in the game; those already sent are deleted on their own after 13 months.
  • Your game: deleting the game deletes it from the device; the iCloud copy is deleted in the iCloud settings.
  • Advertising data: change your choices in Google's window; your rights are exercised with Google, at myaccount.google.com.

You can also write to us at any time at legionduparadis@gmail.com; we reply within 5 working days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date. A change in what the game sends would be announced in the game before taking effect.
