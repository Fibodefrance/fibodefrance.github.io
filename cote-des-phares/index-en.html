Lighthouse Coast

A gentle game of lighthouses to relight, on iPhone and iPad. By Chouette Games.

The last keeper left twenty years ago; since then, the boats no longer come home. Relight the lighthouses of four coasts, one by one: they guide the boats, which bring back the oil for the next lighthouse. Keepers return, letters arrive every day, and the lighthouses keep watch while the game is closed.

Frequently asked questions

Do my lighthouses keep working when the game is closed?

Yes: they keep watch for up to 8 hours at first, then longer thanks to the keeper's house and to Maëlle. Nothing is lost: when you come back, tap "Collect".

How do I find my game on a new iPhone or iPad?

Your game is kept on your iCloud account. On the new device, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your coast comes back on its own (it may take a minute). If the new device already has a game started, the game asks you which one to keep.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close the game completely, then open it again while online: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered when the game reopens. For the assistant keeper and the no-ads pass, the shop also has a "Restore purchases" button. If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment data). For a refund, Apple decides: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Evening fires, storms and letters

The evening fires can be lit once a day, from 6 pm to midnight. The first storm comes with the 2nd lighthouse, then one on Saturday and one on Sunday. A letter arrives every day; earlier ones wait for you.

How do I change the language, or turn off the sound or reminders?

Sound: the foghorn button at the top of the screen. Language, reminders and statistics: tap the keeper's portrait at the top of the screen; the buttons are at the bottom of the keepers window.

How do I turn off game statistics?

Keepers window → "Statistics: off". See the privacy policy.

Ads

There are only ads if you choose them: to double the harvest from your time away, or for the cargo from the open sea in the shop. Never a banner, never a forced ad. The no-ads pass gives these gifts without any ad.
