The studio

Chouette Games was born in 2026, in France. Behind the owl is David Scalisi. Each game takes a minute to learn and is cared for in the details: beautiful pictures, a story, and no pressure.

We love games you can pick up and put down whenever you like: solitaire on a night train, fishing at sunrise, a mystery in a 1920s palace hotel.
