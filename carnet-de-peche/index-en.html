Misty Lake

A calm fishing game for iPhone and iPad. By Chouette Games.

Your grandfather has handed you his old fishing logbook. The lake, the river, then the sea: sixty fish, each at its own hour, at the real time of day. And a letter in his hand every five pages.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my logbook back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Your logbook is kept on your iCloud account. On the new device, sign in to the same Apple account, install the game and open it: your logbook comes back on its own (it can take a minute). If the new device already has a logbook in progress, the game shows you both and asks which one to keep.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game completely, then reopen it while connected to the Internet: a purchase that was paid but not yet delivered is delivered when the game reopens. You can also tap "Restore my purchases" (the logbook → Cabin → Decorate). If nothing arrives, write to us with the date of the purchase (without any payment details). Refunds are decided by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

A fish won't bite

Every fish has its moment (dawn, daytime, dusk, night), its weather (sun, rain, mist) and sometimes its season. The logbook page tells you when to look for it. The moonfish only comes out on full-moon nights. Worms are free and unlimited.

How do I change the language, or turn off sound or reminders?

Open the logbook (top right of the lake screen), then tap Settings: sound, music, vibrations, reminders, statistics and language.

How do I turn off game statistics?

The logbook → Settings → Statistics: "Off". See the privacy policy.

The weekly contest

Every week, one fish for everyone: your finest catch counts. Size never depends on gear, and only the first three catches of the day count: nobody can buy a win. Your name on the board is drawn at random (an animal, a word, a number); you can change it.

Ads

There are only ads if you choose them: to double the coins for a fish you release, or for the bait of the day. Never banners, never forced ads.
